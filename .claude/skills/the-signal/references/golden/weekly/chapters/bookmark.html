• A stormlight-scale epic, book one

    Fantasy · doorstopper · start of a series

    Hard-magic worldbuilding with an actual system you could diagram, and a broken protagonist worth 1,200 pages. If you loved the Cosmere's scale, this is the on-ramp. No spoilers — just start it on a long weekend.

  • Malazan-adjacent, but kinder to newcomers

    Fantasy · military · ensemble cast

    All the grim grandeur and convergence-of-armies sweep, with a narrative that trusts you without drowning you. The kind of book that rewards a notebook. Bring patience; it pays it back.

  • A lean sci-fi palate cleanser

    Sci-fi · standalone · one sitting

    After a thousand-page epic, this 300-page first-contact story is a glass of cold water — one idea, chased to its end, beautifully. Finish it before the next brick lands.
