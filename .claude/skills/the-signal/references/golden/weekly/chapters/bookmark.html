• The Eye of the World

    Fantasy · doorstopper · start of a 14-book world

    Robert Jordan's staggering scale and bottomless lore are exactly the fix for the "lose yourself for months" itch. And there's a neat symmetry for a Cosmere reader: Brandon Sanderson literally finished the series after Jordan's death. No spoilers — just clear a few weekends.

  • The Black Company

    Fantasy · military · morally grey

    Glen Cook's ensemble mercenary fantasy is the book that directly inspired Malazan — the single most on-target pick for a Malazan lover who somehow hasn't read it yet. Grim, lean, and told from the ranks looking up.

  • All Systems Red

    Sci-fi · novella · one sitting

    Martha Wells' first Murderbot Diary is a sardonic, anxious security android that would far rather watch soap operas than talk to humans. A Hugo- and Nebula-winning palate cleanser to slip between the epic bricks.
