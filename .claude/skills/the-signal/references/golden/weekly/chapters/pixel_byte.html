    Palworld hits 1.0

    The "Pokémon-with-guns" survival-crafter finally leaves early access after roughly two and a half years, and 1.0 is not a token stamp — it is the largest update in the game's history, a changelog said to run to 27 pages. The map roughly doubles: a set of Paldium-suspended sky islands floats above Palpagos, and a new World Tree endgame region caps the reworked story. There is a Wing Pack for proper aerial traversal (so flight no longer costs you a Pal slot in combat), two new systems in Awakening and Mutation, and dozens of new Pals pushing the roster towards 300. It is free for existing players and runs a treat on Steam Deck. All of this arrives with a Nintendo and Pokémon Company patent suit still live in the background — though the case has gone Pocketpair's way lately, with patent offices in both Japan and the US knocking back key claims, and no injunction granted. A viral hit shepherded to a genuinely finished state. Back on the wishlist.

    Black Flag, resynced

    Assassin's Creed Black Flag Resynced (9 July) is a ground-up remake of 2013's much-loved pirate adventure, rebuilt on the latest Anvil engine with ray-traced lighting and Steam Deck Verified at launch. Fifteen Ubisoft studios worked on it, led by Ubisoft Singapore, and Matt Ryan — Edward Kenway's original voice — returns for new scenes. It sold past two million copies in a single day, which tells you the affection for the original never really faded — Black Flag was always the entry people pointed newcomers towards, the one where the sailing and the shanties outshone the assassinating. New officers for the Jackdaw and extra missions round it out, but the pull is the same as it ever was: a good ship, an open sea, and a horizon to chase. Set sail again.

  • 114.8

    The Switch 2 summer keeps filling

    Digimon Story: Time Stranger arrived on 10 July on both Switch and Switch 2, and it is one of several heavy hitters landing on the platform this month alone. Reviews have been warm — a time-travel plot reckoned among the strongest the series has told, wrapped around familiar scanning and branching Digivolution. On Switch 2 the smart pick is the 60fps Performance Mode; the 4K Quality Mode looks the part but is dogged by frame-pacing and HDR wrinkles. A fine entry point even for newcomers. The backlog groans.

    Housekeeping that matters

    Google's July Pixel update squashes a nasty boot-loop bug affecting the Pixel 6 through 10 — the kind of fault that can brick a phone at the worst moment, so it is worth installing before you travel. On the software side, OpenAI has quietly swapped in GPT-5.5 Instant Mini as ChatGPT's fallback model, the lightweight tier that catches your query when the flagship is busy. Small changes, but the sort that quietly shape the day. Update before you travel.
