FITNESS · BLOCK 3, WEEK ONE

The marathon is done, the holiday's over, and Block 3 opened on 8 July with a different job entirely: build muscle, eating at maintenance to a slight surplus. The temptation in week one is to come out swinging with maximum sets. The volume-landmark model says the opposite — start near your minimum effective volume and climb toward maximum adaptive volume across the block, adding sets week by week. A 2024 Journal of Applied Physiology trial backed this in trained men: progressively increasing weekly sets beat holding volume fixed for hypertrophy.

Muscle is hard to build in a deficit, so eat at maintenance to a slight surplus — roughly 5–10% over — with 1.6–2.2 g of protein per kilo. And coming off a week of active-recovery holiday, the controlled ramp does double duty: it lets the tissue re-adapt before the hard weeks arrive.

DO THIS WEEK · THE SESSION

Log your hard sets per muscle this week and start low — about 10–12 per major muscle — then pencil in +2 sets a week from here.

Coming off holiday, week one is the floor of the ramp, not a test of the ceiling. The block is built by climbing, not by maxing out cold.

The Ledger

MONEY · RATES & THE ALLOWANCE

Cash is still paying properly, if you go looking. As of 10 July the best easy-access rates sit around 4.5% AER — Chase at 4.5% unlimited for new customers, Revolut at 5% until 4 December on up to £25k, Cahoot at 5% on up to £3k. On the tax-free side, the top easy-access cash ISAs pay about 4.51% (Trading 212, new money) and 4.42% (Chip, on transfers). Legacy accounts paying half that are quietly costing real money over a year.

The catch is timing. This year's ISA allowance is £20,000, but MoneySavingExpert reports it is reportedly set to fall to £12,000 from April 2027 — with over-65s keeping the full £20,000. That's reported, not yet legislated, so treat it as a signal rather than settled law.

DO THIS WEEK · THE LEDGER

Check what your easy-access cash is actually earning; if it's under ~4.5%, move it — and shelter what you can in a top cash ISA while this year's £20k allowance stands.

Rates are variable and several carry bonus cliffs, so diarise a review. With the allowance reportedly shrinking from April 2027, tax-free space is worth using now, not later.
