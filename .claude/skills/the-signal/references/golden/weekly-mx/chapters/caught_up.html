1. Westminster. Andy Burnham is declared Labour leader unopposed (17 Jul) and is set to be appointed prime minister on Monday — Britain's seventh leader in a decade, arriving with no general election.

  2. The final. Spain meet Argentina at MetLife at 15:00 ET today, the top two ranked sides in the first all-Spanish-speaking World Cup final since 1930.

  3. Bronze. England beat France 6-4 to take third place at the World Cup (18 Jul).

  4. SW19. Jannik Sinner defends his Wimbledon title over Alexander Zverev (12 Jul); Linda Nosková takes the women's crown, the youngest champion since 2011.

  5. Spa. Kimi Antonelli starts on pole for the Belgian Grand Prix, which lights up at Spa later today.

  6. The Gulf. Washington declares its June ceasefire with Iran over, strikes Iranian targets to protect Strait of Hormuz shipping and imposes fresh sanctions (15 Jul).

  7. The Levant. Israel agrees to withdraw from 'pilot zones' in southern Lebanon under the June ceasefire (15 Jul).

  8. The ledger. Britain's mortgage price war deepens — Nationwide has cut rates four times in a month — ahead of the Bank of England's 30 Jul decision, where a hold at 3.75% is expected.
