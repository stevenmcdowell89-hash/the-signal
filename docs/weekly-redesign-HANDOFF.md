## The situation (why this exists)

The weekly magazine (generated by the `the-signal` skill) has three compounding
problems, surfaced by the owner's real use:
1. **Structure** — the rebuilt four-movement weekly comes out malformed (e.g. the
   2026-07-12 issue shipped ~16 flat sections; The Desk exploded into standalone
   sections; movement bands missing).
2. **Reliability** — nearly every weekly has *some* defect, and the fix loop is a
   slow human back-and-forth, so the owner skips the week. The pipeline has **no
   real quality signal** (SKILL.md admits it) and publish isn't mechanically gated.
3. **Visuals** — the weekly wears the *special edition's* dramatic dark cover every
   week, its "warm paper" is actually cool grey, it carries ~20 clashing section
   palettes, injects ~600KB of special+holiday CSS/fonts, and the recent rebuild
   bolted generic grey "app-card" components on top. An incoherent halfway.

These must be fixed together. The owner is open to a **full ground-up rebuild,
including visuals** — no attachment to the current implementation. Design from the
magazine's **intent**, not from what it currently does.

---

## DECISIONS ALREADY MADE (do not relitigate)

- **Visual direction: "TRANSMISSION" is chosen.** A warm-tech "signal / broadcast"
  identity that leans into the magazine's name — waveform rules as dividers, a
  tuner-style "station list" of coverlines, monospace datalines, band-code section
  eyebrows, hot vermilion + signal-blue on TRUE warm cream, Instrument Serif +
  Newsreader + JetBrains Mono. The Almanac concept's **"The Week in Figures"**
  personal-data ledger is folded in (recoloured to the Transmission palette).
- **The canonical visual target is a built reference issue** (see below) — a
  complete, JS-off weekly in the Transmission identity. Implement *to match it*.
- **Publish policy (from the owner):** the magazine MUST be delivered every Sunday
  (he is the reader, not the editor); quality is paramount; multiple bounded rounds
  of auto-repair are fine. Hold-and-notify only for hard-safety failures.
- **Weekly ≠ special:** the weekly becomes the constant, warm, recognisable object;
  the dark dramatic bound-magazine treatment is reserved for specials (their signal
  of difference).
