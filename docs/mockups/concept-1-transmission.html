BAND 02 — THE LETTER FROM THE EDITOR

A week that turned quietly.

On coronations, champions, and the things that change without a vote.

There are weeks that arrive with a bang and weeks that simply tune in, and this was the latter — a low, steady frequency you only notice once it has already changed the station. A country prepares to swap its prime minister with nobody casting a ballot, and somehow the loudest thing all week was the quiet of it.

So I went looking for the noise elsewhere and found it on grass and gravel: two Wimbledon champions crowned properly, with a crowd; a World Cup narrowing to its final four; Leclerc winning where speed lives. Coronations, it turns out, come in two kinds this week — the sort with trophies, and the sort with none. I've set them side by side and let you decide which felt more real.

— The Editor
