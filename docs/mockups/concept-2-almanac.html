From the Desk

The Letter

Issue Sixteen · Sunday morning

Some weeks arrive quietly and some, like this one, seem determined to be remembered. On Tuesday Britain changed its prime minister without a single ballot being cast — Andy Burnham walking to No. 10 by arithmetic rather than by vote, and the country waking to a new government it did not choose. It is the sort of thing that will read strangely in the record, and stranger still for how little it disturbed the traffic.

Then the weekend, as if to restore some order, wrote its own copy. Two new champions lifted plate at Wimbledon; the World Cup narrowed to its final four; and Leclerc, against the odds and the weather, won at Silverstone. I ran through all of it — week six of the block, thirty-one miles that felt, for once, like progress. A good week to keep. So I have kept it here.

Yours, over coffee,— The Editor
