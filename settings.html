Sources (RSS · HN · Reddit)

OnIDDomainURLWTier
Type is inferred from the URL: an HN Algolia URL → "hn", a reddit.com/r/… URL → "reddit", everything else → "rss". Almost anything with a feed works as a source — a YouTube channel (youtube.com/feeds/videos.xml?channel_id=…), a Google News RSS, a Mastodon .rss, a podcast feed, or a subreddit. Paste the URL, set its domain + weight.
▸ How ranking works — source weight vs interest rating
Two dials decide how high an item sits, and they multiply:

· Interest rating (a topic's Importance, in the Interests tab) — how much a whole subject matters to you. Raising it lifts every item in that subject.
· Source weight (the W column above) — how much you trust one feed. It's a multiplier on only that feed's items.

Roughly: score ≈ (topic relevance + source popularity) × source weight × freshness × register.

So if one feed floods the brief (e.g. a high-volume source like Football Italia keeps filling the top), lower its W — that dampens just that feed, not the whole sport. To lift or sink a whole subject, change its Importance instead. (A single source also can't fill more than a few slots in any one section.)

Topic rules — suppress & downrank

Per-topic filters. Suppress drops a matching item (book/film spoilers, fitness diet talk, finance hype); Downrank keeps it but pushes it down (e.g. UK process-politics in World). Words are comma-separated, matched in the headline.
