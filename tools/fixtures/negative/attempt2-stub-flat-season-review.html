II

How Forty-Eight Works

The expansion was not just more teams; it was a different shape. Twelve groups of four replaced the old eight, and the arithmetic of qualification changed with it — the eight best third-placed sides advance, so a single point can matter three games later.

The 48-team format · in brief

GROUPS
12 groups of four; top two plus the eight best thirds advance
KNOCKOUT
A new round of 32 before the familiar last-16 spine
HOSTS
The United States, Canada and Mexico — the first three-nation edition
FINAL
MetLife Stadium, East Rutherford, New Jersey

More teams did not dilute the tournament so much as lengthen its middle — the group stage became a competition of its own.

The Road Through the Rounds

The knockout path gained a rung. Where thirty-two nations once meant a straight run of last-16, quarters, semis and final, forty-eight added a round of 32 at the front — one more night where a favourite could fall.
